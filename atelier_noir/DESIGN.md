## Brand & Style

This design system channels high-fashion editorial minimalism, positioned at the intersection of European runway severity and Moroccan sartorial heritage. The identity evokes the quiet confidence of contemporary architectural tailoring—austere, intentional, and tactually rich.

### Target Audience & Tone

The audience is the modern North African and international man who seeks elevated essentials, fluid linen tailoring, deconstructed djellaba-inspired silhouettes, and structured outerwear. The emotional tone is authoritative, understated, and pristine. Interactions should feel deliberate and calm, eschewing aggressive commercial cues in favor of galleried contemplation.

### Design Movement: Warm Monochromatic Editorial

Drawing inspiration from Parisian luxury houses and architectural journals, the aesthetic pairs razor-sharp architectural layouts with warm, tactile material tones. Layouts rely on high-tension negative space, monolithic portrait imagery, microscopic hairline dividers, and disciplined typographic scale. Structural clarity takes precedence over ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in deep noir, chalky alabaster, and desert-stone minerality. It operates primarily in an elevated warm-light canvas, supported by an inverted deep-noir surface system for high-impact editorial moments and night capsules.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces

- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F8F6` (Alabaster Warm) provides an unbleached, high-end paper finish that softens high-contrast typography.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#F4F2EE` (Raw Plaster) is used for secondary panels, drawers, and modal containers.
- **Pristine White:** `#FFFFFF` is reserved strictly for isolated image stage backgrounds and input fields to maximize garment silhouette crispness.

### Deep Monochromatic Noir

- **True Noir:** `#0F0F10` serves as primary text, primary button backgrounds, and deep display elements.
- **Carbon:** `#18181B` forms structural frames, dark mode canvases, and secondary brand tiles.
- **Muted Zinc:** `#27272A` and `#3F3F46` define low-emphasis text, secondary icons, and muted metadata.

### Sand & Stone Accents

- **High Stone:** `#A19B91` provides refined accentuation, used for currency qualifiers, active metadata, and delicate borders.
- **Muted Sand:** `#D8D2C7` and `#E5E0D8` serve as hairline separators, swatch rings, and structural line work.

### Rules of Engagement

- Never introduce saturated promotional colors (e.g., bright red for sales). Markdowns and notices rely exclusively on italicized editorial serif typography, uppercase stone tags, or inverted noir badges.
- Dividers must always use a hairline stroke (`#E5E0D8` on light surfaces; `#27272A` on dark surfaces).

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances the dramatic optical contrast of **Bodoni Moda** with the crystalline, utilitarian discipline of **Hanken Grotesk**.

### Editorial Display Hierarchies

- High-level collection titles, campaign declarations, and article headings use Bodoni Moda at weight 400. In large headings, tight tracking (`-0.03em`) lends an austere, couture demeanor.
- Italic variants of Bodoni Moda denote artistic subheadings, garment material origins (e.g., _Sourced from the Atlas foothills_), and seasonal curations.

### Functional Sans-Serif Structure

- Body text, specifications, tabular data, and UI interactions utilize Hanken Grotesk. Light weights (300/400) maintain an airy editorial feel.
- Uppercase styling combined with extreme letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.22em`) is required for buttons, status chips, table headers, and category navigation.

### Moroccan Dirham (`MAD` / `DH`) Formatting

- Currency amounts must be rendered with modern sans-serif precision. Use the format `1,850 DH` or `1 850 MAD`, placing the currency code in medium weight with a non-breaking space after the numeric value. Never display currency as superscript or miniature fractions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural, asymmetric fluid-grid framework inspired by print publication spreads. Generous whitespace ensures each product garment reads as a collectible art object.

### Breakpoints and Grid Structure

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Product listings resolve to a strict single or high-fashion 2-column alternating cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 40px margins, 24px gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, 64px max-edge margins, 40px gutters. Content reaches a maximum reading width of 1600px with generous lateral air.

### Editorial Composition Patterns

- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero sections place headline typography directly overlapping or staggered adjacent to 3:4 aspect-ratio photography.
- **The Hairline Matrix:** Column divisions in product grids and header bars feature microscopic 1px boundaries (`#E5E0D8`) that define space without adding heavy visual weight.
- **Component Padding:** Inner component padding remains compact and disciplined (`space-sm` to `space-md`), while margins between disparate sections expand dramatically (`space-xl` and above) to cultivate an unhurried browsing tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects conventional drop shadows, colored glows, and blurred skeuomorphism. Spatial layering is achieved entirely through planar separation, tonal layering, and ultra-crisp hairline contours.

### Planar Separation

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base raw alabaster `#F9F8F6` creates the foundational substrate.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` lifts cards and product canvases subtly without casting shadows.
- **Level 2 (Drawers, Overlays & Sticky Trays):** Elevated panels utilize `#FFFFFF` or deep `#0F0F10`, bounded strictly by a 1px solid stroke in `#E5E0D8` or `#27272A`.

### Dynamic Depth & Scrim

- Flyout checkout drawers and quick-view modals dim the foundational viewport using an austere 60% opacity carbon veil (`#0F0F10` with `backdrop-blur-sm`).
- Hover states transition seamlessly through soft tone fills rather than vertical translation or cast shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system is uncompromisingly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Curved geometries clash with the architectural rigor of luxury tailoring and modern editorial typography.

### Geometries & Bounds

- **Buttons, Containers, Inputs & Badges:** Must maintain absolute 90-degree right angles (`0px` border-radius).
- **Media Containers:** Product imagery and campaign video containers must use razor-sharp edges with strict 4:5 (portrait) or 16:9 (cinematic) aspect ratios.
- **Exceptions:** Circular indicators are permitted solely for circular color swatches and minimal status dots, ensuring they register as pure geometric circles rather than rounded rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls

- **Primary CTA:** Solid noir (`#0F0F10`) background, crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) text, uppercase `label-caps` typography, 0px radius, 52px height. On hover: transitions to `#27272A` with subtle letter-spacing expansion.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent background, 1px perimeter border in `#0F0F10`, text in `#0F0F10`. Hover transitions to solid `#0F0F10` fill with inverted text.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Unbordered, underlined with a 1px solid hairline offset by 4px.

### Product Cards

- **Structure:** 4:5 aspect ratio image frame in `#FFFFFF` with minimal 1px `#E5E0D8` bottom edge. Product title in Bodoni Moda (`headline-sm` or `title-editorial`), subtitle/category in muted charcoal, and price cleanly formatted in `Hanken Grotesk` (`1,200 DH`).
- **Interaction:** Hovering initiates a crossfade to secondary garment detail or lookbook shot. A minimal "QUICK VIEW +" text trigger surfaces along the lower boundary with zero vertical displacement.

### Trust Badges & Local Utility (Morocco)

- **Cash on Delivery (Paiement à la Livraison):** Rendered as an unbordered minimal slate pill or structural hairline cell. Employs `label-micro` typography alongside a razor-thin monoline icon.
- **National Delivery Badge:** "LIVRAISON PARTOUT AU MAROC — 24/48H" formatted in `label-micro` uppercase tracking, anchored in an understated stone band (`#F4F2EE`).

### Selectors (Size & Color Swatches)

- **Size Selector:** Grid of square tiles (44px x 44px), 0px corner radius, hairline border `#E5E0D8`. Active state switches to solid `#0F0F10` with `#FFFFFF` text. Unavailable sizes display an ultra-fine diagonal strike-through stroke.
- **Color Selector:** 20px circular pigment swatches surrounded by a 2px offset whitespace ring, encircled by a 1px noir hairline when active.

### Drawers & Navigation

- **Cart / Quick View Drawer:** Slides flush from the right screen boundary, 460px fixed width on desktop, 100% width on mobile. Outlined with a left 1px hairline border in `#E5E0D8`. Free-shipping threshold meter rendered as an austere 2px black line across an `#E5E0D8` track.
- **Header Navigation:** Ultra-lean sticky navigation with minimal height (64px), centered brand mark, left-aligned collection routes in `label-caps`, right-aligned search, account, and bag indicators.
